I have always wished for a house with big windows. In fact, why have we even trapped ourselves in boxes of concrete and glass for that matter? Nature was supposed to be free, now they are scheduled events that we experience when we go on treks or camping, or manage somehow with the pitiful state of the nearby polluted park walks. With the current limitations of society and environment, I still try to meekly observe it. I feel lucky to have left a dreadful, extreme weathers and a polluted city and shift to a little more nature friendly and conscious city where the wea ther motivates you to go out and seek nature.
I'm also lucky to find a home on the 16th floor here with a huge multi-wall spanning east side window. That has made a drastic difference in my lifestyle. The natural lights regulates my sleep cycle and auto corrects it. Also the view is a sight to behold and can keep you engaged for hours if you let it. Here's a picture! The picture wouldn't be able to do much justice as it doesnt capture the dynamicity as well as the intricacy of it. The main things that you should notice is the lake at a distance where you sometimes observe large migratory birds moving, 
the railway tracks (I like the indian railway system, there are a variety of trains and also a variety of activities happening around the tracks), the under construction buildings where you see floors rising every month, and most importantly the birds flying at an arm's distance from you. I'll dedicate this blog to them.

A small brownish black bird with 2 white spots in its tail flies gracefully and displays a lot of stunts and movements, as if it was giving me a show. I have named some of their stunts - The rocket twirl, the pendulum swing and the standard three revolution loop. <insert magpie-drawing>
They are the oriental magpie robins and they often show off their skills to me. <insert image magpie-collage>

While I sip my tea and hang a bit outside of the window, I could see them coming very close to me, acknowledging my presence.  I have named some of them, based on their groups and the floow height in which they fly. The one that flies at my floor has been named Biscuit. He (I dont know why but it felt like it was a he) is a lone bird, who doesnt care about the other birds, he enjoys his own flight, tries the most riskiest stunts hoping that someone would be noticing and praising him. Then there is romeo and juliet on the 14th floor, they always fly together and rest together. Biscuit sometimes tries to third weel. I havent yet named the adhoc guests or probably the kids of romeo and juliet, they definitely have atleast one kid, as I have clicked their family picture.

I also have seen only a handful of times bald eagles, flying very high and making all the other birds terrified. Once I saw a huge bird at a very far distance, Probably the biggest flying bird I've seen free. <insert image bigbird-drawing>
I so wish I could fly like them, at least once, one flight. <insert image quote>

Also obviously there are a dozen of pestering pigeons all over the floors. One of them who I have named "roommate" is a permanent resident of my balcony. Its favourite hobby is shitting on my plants. 

Not all birds are happy. I am greeted daily with the most devastating and loud cries of a Sun Conure on the 14th floor of the tower in front of me. Its a pet bird trapped within a balcony net. Oh I have name it "Rajkumar Rao" and I had written something for it. I feel sorry for you, but there's nothing I can do. All I can say is to be patient. Dont get envious of those free flying birds. Maybe in another life you will get to fly high like them. Dont give youself more pain, sometimes I could feel the your pain in my throat. However you will have to accept that life is unfair and that's alright.
